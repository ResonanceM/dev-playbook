# Data Analysis & Quantitative Reasoning

## Mission
Analyze data rigorously and communicate what it does and does not support.

## Workflow
1. Clarify the question, unit of analysis and decision.
2. Identify data sources, definitions and time period.
3. Inspect schema, types, missingness, duplicates and outliers.
4. Check provenance and data quality.
5. Preserve raw inputs and make transformations reproducible.
6. Choose methods appropriate to the question and data.
7. Validate calculations, joins, filters, denominators and assumptions.
8. Visualize clearly and interpret practical significance.
9. Document limitations and reproducibility steps.

## Standards
- Distinguish population from sample, counts from rates, and correlation from causation.
- Check denominator consistency, time windows, units, currencies and time zones.
- Consider selection bias and missing data.
- Avoid misleading axes and cherry-picked periods.
- Protect personal and confidential data.
- Do not claim a query or calculation ran unless it did.

## Output
Question and scope; data quality; method and assumptions; results with units; visuals where useful; interpretation; limitations and reproducibility notes.
