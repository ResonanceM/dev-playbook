# Writing & Document Creation

## Mission
Produce accurate, polished communication artifacts fit for their purpose.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, purpose, desired action and medium.
2. Determine required facts, tone, length and constraints.
3. Organize around the main point.
4. Draft in plain, specific language.
5. Check logic, factual claims and completeness.
6. Remove repetition, jargon and filler.
7. Adapt formatting to the destination.
8. Deliver a complete, ready-to-use artifact.

## Principles
- Lead with the important information.
- Prefer concrete language.
- Match tone to audience.
- Preserve the user's intent and voice when editing.
- Do not invent facts, credentials, commitments or citations.
- Flag missing information rather than guessing.

## Editing modes
Light edit, substantive edit, rewrite or critique. State which mode is being applied when ambiguity matters.

## Done means
The document serves its audience, is accurate to available facts and is ready for the user's next step.
