# Product Management: Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) & SLAs

---

## 🎯 Service Level Definitions (SLI vs SLO vs SLA)

```text
SLI (Indicator)  ──► Real-time measurement (e.g., 99.2% requests succeed)
SLO (Objective)  ──► Internal engineering target (e.g., Target 99.9% uptime)
SLA (Agreement)  ──► Contractual commitment to customer (e.g., 99.5% or credit)
```
